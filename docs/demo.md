# Demo script

The exact clicks for the judges, under 3 minutes. (Draft; finalized in the demo task.)

1. **Pitch (20 s):** "When you build with Claude, it quietly makes dozens of assumptions. MindCode shows you every one of them."
2. Type: *"I want a chat app for my study group."* The map grows: `Chat app` and `Study group` in blue (user), `Message API` and `Real-time updates` in amber (assumed), `How do users log in?` in red (unknown).
3. Point out light vs deep amber: an obvious inference vs a creative leap.
4. Click `How do users log in?`, type "just a nickname, no passwords", and Claude rewrites the bubble. Click **Approve**, and it turns green.
5. Click **Build**. The app appears in the preview.
6. Hover the page title: the `Chat app` and `Study group` bubbles light up. Click `Nickname login`: the login box is outlined in the preview.
7. Show the checkpoint timeline: click the first checkpoint to see the map before approval.
8. (If shipped) the "Needs your attention" list and a locked bubble surviving a rebuild.
9. **Close (15 s):** "You keep your full sentences; Claude shows its reasoning as a structure you can approve, and the code follows the structure."
